# ResumeLingo — "Full Circle" Walkthrough Video Script & Shot List

Sep 25, 2026 · CJ Owens

## Overview

**Purpose:** a walkthrough video of ResumeLingo's "Full Circle" loop (Apply → Interview → Get hired → Stay current) for YouTube — top-of-funnel discovery, and an embeddable asset for the marketing site. Grounded directly in the copy and routes already live on `/full-circle`, so nothing here is aspirational or needs UI built to match it.

**Target runtime:** ~3 minutes. Tutorial content drops off sharply after the first minute or two, so this stays tight — one clear beat per stage rather than a comprehensive feature tour.

**Open production decision:** record as one continuous take with voiceover laid on top afterward, or record stage-by-stage as four separate clips and stitch them together. Stage-by-stage is easier to reshoot if one section needs a retake, and makes it simpler to reuse individual stage clips later (e.g. a 15-second "Ask Poly" clip as its own short-form piece) — recommended unless there's a strong reason to want one unbroken take.

**Demo data:** use one clean demo account throughout, not a real user's resume or job search — same authenticity standard already flagged for the marketing site's testimonials (App Improvements, seed-1).

## Cold Open (0:00–0:15)

**Screen:** dashboard, logged into the demo account, cursor idle for a beat before anything happens.

**Voiceover:** "Most resume tools stop once you hit download. ResumeLingo doesn't — it follows you from your first application to your next promotion. Here's the whole loop, in under three minutes."

## Stage 1 — Apply (0:15–1:00)

**Screen:** `/resumes/new` — show Poly asking a profession-specific question (pick something visually clear: a certifications question or a GitHub-link question), then a generated bullet appearing on screen.

**Voiceover:** "It starts with the resume. Poly asks the right questions for your field — certifications, clinical experience, CI/CD, whatever applies — and drafts bullets about impact, not just duties."

**Screen:** cut to the Job Tracker, logging a new application (company + status).

**Voiceover:** "Once you've applied, log it in the Job Tracker, so you're never guessing what you sent where."

## Stage 2 — Interview (1:00–1:45)

**Screen:** `/career-coach` — ask Poly a mock interview question, show a response on screen.

**Voiceover:** "Got an interview? Ask Poly to run through likely questions for the role, or help you structure a STAR answer."

**Screen:** cut to a generated Cover Letter.

**Voiceover:** "And walk in with a tailored cover letter as your table-setter — built straight from the resume you already have."

## Stage 3 — Get Hired (1:45–2:15)

**Screen:** `/thank-you-letter` — generate a thank-you note same-day.

**Voiceover:** "Send a thank-you letter the same day, while you're still top of mind."

**Screen:** the resume's public share link (one URL, not a re-attached PDF).

**Voiceover:** "And because your resume lives at one link, updating it later doesn't mean tracking down every place you ever sent a PDF."

## Stage 4 — Stay Current (2:15–2:40)

**Screen:** the resume refresh nudge — either the email or the in-app nudge card.

**Voiceover:** "A resume that's accurate the day you're hired goes stale fast. ResumeLingo nudges you to add a new role or a fresh achievement — so the next opportunity doesn't start with a scramble to rewrite everything."

## Close / CTA (2:40–3:00)

**Screen:** back to the dashboard, cursor hovering over the loop.

**Voiceover:** "Apply. Interview. Get hired. Stay current. Then it starts again. That's the full circle." [logo] "Build your first resume free at resumelingo.com."

## Production Notes

- **Demo account only** — no real user's resume, job search, or contact info. Build one consistent fictional profile and reuse it across every stage so the story reads as continuous.
- **Every shot maps to a real, working route** (`/resumes/new`, `/career-coach`, `/thank-you-letter`, the Job Tracker, the resume refresh nudge) — nothing here is a mockup or a UI that doesn't exist yet, so there's no faked screen to keep in sync with the product later.
- **Recording approach:** stage-by-stage is recommended over one continuous take — easier to reshoot a single section, and individual clips (e.g. the "Ask Poly" moment) can be repurposed later as short-form content.
- **Before recording:** confirm the Full Circle loop is stable under real use (it's a newer feature, shipped behind the `CAREER_LOOP_ENABLED` flag) — a video pointing viewers at a rough edge undermines the pitch it's making.
- **Voiceover timing** above is approximate — read each line aloud once at a natural pace before locking screen-capture length to it, rather than cutting the recording to a guessed time and fitting narration afterward.
